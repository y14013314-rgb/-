PRIVACY INFORMATION

Your travel inquiry.

How we use information you choose to send to SUKHA.

Information you provide

The request builder asks for your name and travel preferences. It creates a summary in your browser; reviewing or copying it does not send an inquiry or save it to the website database. If you choose “Submit to SUKHA” or the online submission option, we save your name, email, request summary and consent in our website database and send a notification to our business team. Please do not enter passport numbers, payment-card information or sensitive personal details.

Sending your request

You can submit online after reviewing your details, or choose WhatsApp to open a pre-filled message, or copy the summary and paste it into WeChat. You must send the message in the selected service for SUKHA to receive it. Opening WhatsApp passes the summary to that service; WhatsApp and WeChat process information under their own privacy policies.

How we use your message

When you send us a request, our team uses the information to respond, prepare a proposal and follow up on your travel arrangements. No marketing subscription is created by the request builder.

Contact and requests

Contact devin@sukhatrip.com to ask about your information or request correction or deletion of information you have sent to SUKHA.

Payments

The request builder does not take payment or confirm a booking. Availability, itinerary, quotation and booking terms must be confirmed with our team.
