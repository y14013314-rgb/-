PRIVACY INFORMATION

Your travel inquiry.

How we use information you choose to send to SUKHA.

Information you provide

The inquiry form collects your name, email and travel preferences, plus optional contact details. We use this information to respond, prepare proposals and follow up on your requested travel arrangements. Please do not submit passport numbers, payment-card information or sensitive personal details.

Storage and team access

Submitted inquiries and related notes and quotations are stored in the website’s hosted database. Only authorized team accounts may access the management system. The hosting platform processes data to operate this service.

Contact and requests

Contact SUKHA at Email · Coming soon / 暂未开通 to request correction, deletion or information about your inquiry. Records remain until reviewed and deleted by the operator; automatic retention deletion is not yet configured. No marketing subscription is created by the inquiry form.

Private quote links

Anyone with your complete private quotation link can view and accept that proposal. Keep the link private and ask your travel designer to revoke it if it has been shared accidentally.

Notifications and payments

Inquiry email notifications are not yet enabled. The website does not collect card or wallet credentials, and quotation acceptance does not confirm payment. The payment process is separate from the inquiry form.
