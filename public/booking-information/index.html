Before any booking or payment.

Your contracting company＋

The full legal company name, travel business credentials, address and responsible service provider must be supplied before booking. These details are pending owner confirmation.

Your proposal & quotation＋

The confirmed route, dates, hotels, transport, guide language, meals and activities should be documented, along with exclusions and optional charges.

Payment & currency＋

A formal proposal must specify the payee, currency, approved payment channel, deposit and balance schedule. This preview accepts no payments.

Changes, cancellation & refunds＋

Terms must match the confirmed products and operators, including train and cruise conditions. No cancellation or refund policy is implied by this preview.

Travel support＋

Confirm how to contact your travel designer before departure and the actual support arrangements during your trip. No response-time or around-the-clock service promise is made here.
