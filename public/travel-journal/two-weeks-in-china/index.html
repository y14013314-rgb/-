THE CHINA JOURNAL · ITINERARIES

Two weeks. A China that feels like you.

A thoughtful starting point for your own journey.

Choose a theme, not a checklist

Fourteen days can support many different journeys. A first encounter with China, a culture-focused Silk Road trip or a city-and-river combination will each use that time differently.

Balance icons with quieter days

Place a slower morning after an active day, and leave room for the experiences that cannot be rushed. Your preferred rhythm should shape the route.

Connect places thoughtfully

Transport availability and transfer times need to be checked for your dates. Avoid assuming that every destination can be added without changing the pace.

Use an itinerary as an invitation

Start with a suggested route, then adapt the stops, stays and activities. The final plan should feel personal rather than exhaustive.
