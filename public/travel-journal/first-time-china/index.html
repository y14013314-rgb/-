THE CHINA JOURNAL · PLANNING

Your first China journey, thoughtfully planned

A thoughtful starting point for your own journey.

Begin with your interests

Think about the moments you want from China: cultural landmarks, local food, contemporary cities, nature or shared time with family. Let those interests guide the route.

Give the journey room to breathe

A shorter list of places can leave more time to experience each one. Consider travel between destinations as part of the itinerary, rather than treating every day as a sightseeing day.

Choose a starting point

A Beijing, Xi’an and Shanghai concept offers one way to begin. A route through Chengdu, Chongqing and the Yangtze creates a different balance. Both should be adjusted to your dates and pace.

Confirm the practical details

Before booking, review access requirements, accommodation, transfers, service language and the exact terms of the proposal. Entry rules should always be checked against current official guidance.
