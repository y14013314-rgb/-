What to expect on shore

Downstream · 4 days

Embark in Chongqing. Explore Fuling and Zhongxian, sail through the gorges, and choose between Wushan’s Goddess Sky Road or the Lesser Gorges. Finish in Yichang with a choice of a Three Gorges Dam visit or an eastern Xiling Gorge boat trip.

Upstream · 5 days

Start in Yichang, then explore Badong or Shennongjia according to your chosen route. Continue to Fengjie and Zhongxian before disembarking in Chongqing. Shore options include historical sites and walking routes.

Round trip · 8 days

Combine the upstream and downstream river landscapes on a voyage returning to your starting city. Select Badong or Shennongjia on the Yichang departures, or Badong on the Chongqing departures shown here.

Excursion choices depend on the sailing. Ship-lift passage is subject to operation and maintenance. Tell us your interests and mobility needs so we can confirm suitable arrangements.

Fares, promotions & booking conditions

Fares depend on route, suite, occupancy and traveller eligibility. The operator’s autumn promotion for bookings from 23 October to 25 November 2026 is restricted to specified Chinese identity and travel documents, including a Chinese permanent residence ID for foreign nationals. Ordinary foreign passports are not eligible. We will check any applicable offer individually.

Children occupying the first or second place in a suite are charged the same fare as adults under the supplied policy. Solo occupancy, a third guest and infants have separate pricing. Please include children’s ages in your enquiry.

The supplied 2027 policy lists cancellation charges of 10% at 180 or more days before departure; 20% at 60–179 days; 50% at 30–59 days; and 100% within 29 days. Ask for the complete terms applicable to your quotation before confirming.

Your written quotation will specify meals, shore activities, transfers, language services, optional extras and payment or amendment conditions. Published schedules do not guarantee availability.
