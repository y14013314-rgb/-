The supplied 2027 policy requires an additional CNY 150 service fee per overseas guest, including children, excluding Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan. Cabin fares, solo supplements, children’s charges and amendment terms will be itemised in your quotation. Ship-lift visits are subject to operation and maintenance.

Ports, timings and shore visits can change with river conditions, weather, navigation controls or operator arrangements. Ask us about accessibility, walking requirements and language services before confirming.
