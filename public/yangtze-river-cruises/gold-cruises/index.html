THE CLASSIC THREE GORGES

A voyage to make your own.

2027 programme · Published dates are supplied for Gold 3, 6 and 8. Gold 2 and 5 dates are on request.

Your ship, your cabin

Deluxe twin cabins on Gold 2, 3, 5 and 6 are listed at 24 m² with a 2.7 m² balcony; Gold 8 lists 27 m² with a 3 m² balcony. Layouts and higher categories vary by vessel.

Classic sights

The 2027 programme includes Fengdu Ghost City, a sightseeing-boat trip through the Lesser Three Gorges and the Three Gorges Dam. Additional excursions remain optional.

Considered upgrades

Executive rooms and suites offer different services and dining arrangements. We will confirm the specific vessel and category before you book.
