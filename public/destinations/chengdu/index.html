A SENSE OF PLACE

Pandas, teahouses and everyday pleasures.

In Chengdu, the small rituals matter: a cup of tea, a neighborhood stroll, a shared Sichuan meal. Make room for the giant pandas and a day outside the city, while keeping the journey comfortably unhurried.

Pandas, tea culture & Dujiangyan

    Arrive & settle in

    Take a gentle neighborhood walk and begin with a Sichuan meal adjusted to your preferences.

    Giant pandas & city life

    Visit a panda conservation facility, then keep the afternoon easy with time in the city.

    Tea & cultural neighborhoods

    Spend time in a teahouse and explore streets such as Kuanzhai or the Wuhou–Jinli area.

    Dujiangyan day out

    Consider the historic irrigation system and surrounding town, allowing for transport and walking time.

    Keep the day flexible for personal interests and onward travel.
