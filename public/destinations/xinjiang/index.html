A SENSE OF PLACE

Oasis cities and Silk Road encounters.

Vast distances are part of the Xinjiang experience. This cultural route focuses on Urumqi, Turpan and Kashgar rather than combining every landscape in one trip. Markets, historic sites and daily life provide a starting point for a thoughtful journey.

AN IDEA TO MAKE YOUR OWN

Urumqi, Turpan & Kashgar

A 7-day planning suggestion, adapted from published travel references. Timing, admissions, transport and services will be confirmed in your personal proposal.

Customize this route ↗
  1. DAY 01

    Arrive in Urumqi

    Settle in with a relaxed introduction to the city.

  2. DAY 02

    City history & markets

    Consider the regional museum and bazaar, subject to opening arrangements.

  3. DAY 03

    Continue to Turpan

    Travel to Turpan and explore the karez water system if timing permits.

  4. DAY 04

    Oasis heritage

    Choose Jiaohe or another historic site and plan sightseeing around seasonal temperatures.

  5. DAY 05

    Return & fly to Kashgar

    Allow a transfer day for the journey via Urumqi and a separately confirmed flight.

  6. DAY 06

    Kashgar old town

    Explore old-town streets, workshops and local food with time for conversation and breaks.

  7. DAY 07

    A flexible farewell

    Keep the morning free before onward travel, or add time for a separate regional extension.
