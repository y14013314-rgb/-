A SENSE OF PLACE

Old towns, mountain air, time to linger.

Yunnan rewards a slower journey. Begin in Kunming, follow the route to Dali and Erhai Lake, then continue to Lijiang for old lanes and mountain scenery. Local customs, seasonal conditions and travel time shape the detail.

AN IDEA TO MAKE YOUR OWN

Kunming, Dali & Lijiang

A 7-day planning suggestion, adapted from published travel references. Timing, admissions, transport and services will be confirmed in your personal proposal.

  1. DAY 01

    Kunming arrival

    Settle in and take a relaxed walk around Green Lake if time permits.

  2. DAY 02

    Stone Forest or city discoveries

    Choose the Stone Forest excursion or a city day around your interests and energy.

  3. DAY 03

    Continue to Dali

    Travel to Dali and settle in before an evening walk in the old town.

  4. DAY 04

    Erhai & local life

    Explore part of the lakeshore and nearby settlements without trying to circle the entire lake.

  5. DAY 05

    Dali to Lijiang

    Continue toward Lijiang and leave time to find your bearings in the old town.

  6. DAY 06

    Lijiang at your pace

    Choose old-town lanes, nearby villages or a suitable mountain excursion after reviewing altitude and conditions.

  7. DAY 07

    Onward travel

    Leave space for departure logistics or extend the journey.
