CHOOSE YOUR JOURNEY

Routes & editions.

Tianshan Pastoral · 天山牧歌版＋

The supplied 2027 edition is a 16-day circuit from Chengdu, with flexible route choices within specified sections.

Urumqi · Day 2＋

A: Xinjiang Museum, International Grand Bazaar and a cultural interpretation session. B: Tianchi Lake.

Altay · Days 3–5＋

A: Altay–Hemu Highway, Hemu, Kanas boat trip and Tuvan music. B: Hemu, birch forest, Baihaba and Kanas. Select one route for this section.

Ili & Kuqa · Days 6–10＋

A: Sayram Lake, Nalati and Duku Highway to Kuqa. B: Sayram Lake, Kalajun, Kurdening and Kuqa. C: Yizhao Highway, Zhaosu Jade Lake and Xiata, followed by Sayram Lake and Kuqa. The brochure lists a CNY 6,000 supplement for C; confirm the applicable basis in your quotation.

A ROUTE TO DISCUSS

16 days across China.

Chengdu · Urumqi · Altay · Ili · Kuqa · Kashgar · Chengdu

The 2027 brochures are planning references. Departure dates, railway schedules, stops, hotel substitutions, service languages and availability must be confirmed for your booking. Rail travel is combined with road excursions and hotel stays; the complete journey is not spent on the train.

  1. Days 1–2 · Chengdu & Urumqi

    Board in Chengdu, then choose a cultural introduction to Urumqi or the Tianchi Lake excursion.

  2. Days 3–5 · Altay

    Select a Hemu and Kanas programme, with the route fixed for this section.

  3. Days 6–10 · Ili & Kuqa

    Choose a grassland, forest or mountain-led itinerary, including Kuqa cultural and canyon visits.

  4. Days 11–13 · Kashgar & the Pamirs

    The brochure describes instrument-making at Minyue Village, White Sand Lake, a Tajik family visit, Karakul Lake, Id Kah Mosque and Kashgar Old City.

  5. Days 14–16 · Return journey

    Enjoy time on board before the confirmed Chengdu or Lanzhou arrival and onward transfer arrangements.

The brochure states that the Duku Highway option is available to domestic guests only and cannot operate when the road is closed. Overseas travelers need an alternative route confirmed before booking. The route-C supplement and all access arrangements must be confirmed in the proposal.
