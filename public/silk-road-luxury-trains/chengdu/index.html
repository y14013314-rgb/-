THE EXPERIENCE

Journey highlights.

A Sichuan-inspired train

The handbook describes 18 cars, 54 compartments and a standard capacity of 108 guests, with dining and multifunctional cars.

Two cabin styles

Leisurely Home twin cabins are listed at 8 m²; Poetic Residence double or twin cabins at 10 m² in the 2027 brochure. The handbook describes private bathrooms and mini-bars. Confirm the exact cabin and occupancy before booking.

Life on board

The Golden Panda Restaurant, Jinshihui dining car, Yuefu Hall and Liuyun Pavilion bring together dining, tea, music and cultural activities.

A ROUTE TO DISCUSS

16 days across China.

Chengdu · Lanzhou · Turpan · Kashgar · Kuqa · Ili · Altay · Urumqi

The 2027 brochures are planning references. Departure dates, railway schedules, stops, hotel substitutions, service languages and availability must be confirmed for your booking. Rail travel is combined with road excursions and hotel stays; the complete journey is not spent on the train.

  1. Days 1–3 · Chengdu to Turpan

    Board in Chengdu, with Lanzhou boarding described in the brochure. Enjoy time on board before Turpan’s karez irrigation system and Flaming Mountains.

  2. Days 4–6 · Kashgar & the Pamirs

    Arrive in Kashgar, explore White Sand Lake and Karakul Lake on the Pamir excursion, then Kashgar Old City before continuing to Kuqa.

  3. Days 7–9 · Kuqa & Ili · autumn version

    Kuqa’s canyon and cultural sites lead to Ili and Sayram Lake. This is the autumn sequence; the midsummer version replaces this section with Bayinbuluk and Nalati.

  4. Days 10–13 · Altay & Kanas · autumn version

    Travel via Beitun to Hemu, Kanas and Burqin, with autumn landscapes and village visits as described in the seasonal brochure.

  5. Days 14–16 · Urumqi & return

    Explore Urumqi before the rail return, reaching Lanzhou or Chengdu according to the confirmed schedule.

The midsummer mountain-road section needs an alternative for guests whose nationality or access permission does not allow the published route. Pamir excursions reach approximately 3,300–3,600 m. Discuss route suitability and access before booking.
