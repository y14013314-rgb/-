THE EXPERIENCE

Journey highlights.

Southern China to the Silk Road

Begin in Guangzhou, with a Chongqing city visit before continuing towards Urumqi and Xinjiang.

A literary-inspired setting

The brochure presents a Chinese literary-household design theme, with onboard cultural activities and a Silk Road salon.

Beyond the familiar circuit

The return route includes Korla, Bosten Lake and Golmud’s Qarhan Salt Lake, as described in the 2027 itineraries.

CHOOSE YOUR JOURNEY

Routes & editions.

Golden Autumn · 金风画卷版＋

The Ili B option visits Sayram Lake, Tianshan Flower Sea and Nalati before Kuqa. If the Duku road in option A is closed, the brochure substitutes option B.

Tianshan Edition · 天山锦书版＋

The Ili B option includes Kalajun, light grassland walking and Kurdening. Its A-option fallback describes returning from Nalati to Yining and transferring to Jinxiu Tianfu for Kuqa; confirm this arrangement before booking.

Urumqi & Altay options＋

Choose a Urumqi cultural visit or Tianchi Lake, followed by one of the fixed Hemu/Kanas touring programmes. The alternative Altay route includes Baihaba and the Tiereketi–Jiadengyu road.

Ili & Kuqa options · Days 8–11＋

A includes Sayram Lake, Nalati and the Duku route. B differs between the two editions. C includes Yizhao Highway, Zhaosu Jade Lake, Xiata and Sayram Lake, continuing to Kuqa; the brochure lists a CNY 6,000 per-person supplement.

A ROUTE TO DISCUSS

18 days across China.

Guangzhou · Chongqing · Urumqi · Altay · Ili · Kuqa · Kashgar · Korla · Golmud · Guangzhou

The 2027 brochures are planning references. Departure dates, railway schedules, stops, hotel substitutions, service languages and availability must be confirmed for your booking. Rail travel is combined with road excursions and hotel stays; the complete journey is not spent on the train.

  1. Days 1–3 · Guangzhou, Chongqing & westbound rail

    Board in Guangzhou. The Chongqing programme lists the Three Gorges Museum, local hotpot and the Raffles City viewing platform before the onward rail journey.

  2. Day 4 · Urumqi

    Choose the Xinjiang Museum and Grand Bazaar programme or Tianchi Lake.

  3. Days 5–7 · Altay & Kanas

    Travel to Hemu and Kanas, with village or road options before continuing from Beitun.

  4. Days 8–11 · Ili & Kuqa

    Choose one of the edition-specific grassland and mountain routes.

  5. Days 12–14 · Kashgar & Korla

    White Sand Lake, Taxkorgan, a Tajik family visit, Karakul Lake and Kashgar Old City feature before continuing towards Korla.

  6. Days 15–18 · Korla, Golmud & Guangzhou

    The brochure lists Bayingolin Museum and Bosten Lake, followed by Qarhan Salt Lake at Golmud and the return rail journey to Guangzhou.

The brochures state that the Duku Highway is open to domestic guests only; overseas travelers need an alternative route confirmed. Border-area visits and high-altitude excursions require individual access checks. Cabin dimensions and detailed layouts were not supplied. Some brochure interiors are promotional illustrations rather than verified photographs.
